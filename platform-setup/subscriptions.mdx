# Subscriptions

The **Subscription** page (`Side nav → User menu → Upgrade to Pro`, or `/subscription`) lists every available Aivah plan, summarizes your current plan, and provides quick access to upgrade or cancellation options.

<Frame caption="Subscription page – Free / Mini / Max plans with the monthly/yearly cycle toggle at the top">
  ![Subscription plans](/images/subscription-plans.png)
</Frame>

## Page layout

- **Billing cycle toggle** sits at the top of the page – switch between **Monthly** and **Yearly** pricing.
- **Plan cards** list each plan's price for the active cycle along with the features it unlocks (`5,000 credits per month`, `3 active MCP tools`, etc.).
- **Action button** on each card opens Stripe checkout. After successful checkout your plan is updated automatically and the cards refresh.

## Key Information

- **Plan Name**: e.g. *Team*, *Enterprise*, *Starter*. Use this when communicating with support or finance.
- **Renewal / Expiry Date**: When your plan will renew or terminate. Add this date to your calendar to avoid surprise renewals.
- **Included Credits**: Each plan card shows the included monthly credit allowance – top up any time from [Credits](/platform-setup/credits) if you exceed it.

## Actions Available

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Switch Billing Cycle" icon="repeat">
    Use the toggle at the top of the page to compare monthly vs yearly pricing. Yearly plans typically include a discount.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Upgrade Plan" icon="arrow-up">
    Click the action button on any higher-tier card to open Stripe checkout. Plan changes apply automatically when checkout completes.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Cancel Subscription" icon="ban">
    Appears when your plan allows cancellation from the interface. Follow the prompts to finish the process or contact support if the option is hidden.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Manage Payment Methods" icon="credit-card">
    Open the [Billing dashboard](/platform-setup/billing) in a new tab to update cards, download invoices, and adjust contacts.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Tip>If you don't see the plan you expect, refresh the page or contact support—plan changes may take a minute to propagate.</Tip>

## When to Use This Page

- **Budget Reviews**: Confirm renewal timing and costs before quarter-end.
- **Scaling Up**: Upgrade ahead of major launches or team expansions to prevent plan limits (such as MCP active tools or shared agents) from blocking work.
- **Pausing Service**: Initiate cancellations or downgrades well before renewal to avoid automatic charges.

## Payment status page

After Stripe checkout you're redirected to `/payment` with one of two states:

- **Success** – animated check mark and a *Return to subscription* CTA.
- **Cancel / Error** – animated X mark with a *Try again* CTA.

If you arrive without a Stripe session id the page simply offers links back to **Home** or **View plans**.

## Related Tasks

- Update payment methods in the [Billing dashboard](/platform-setup/billing) before upgrading.
- Top up [Credits](/platform-setup/credits) if a campaign or launch will exceed your plan's included allowance.
- Use the [Documentation tour](/platform-setup/documentation) to onboard new team members after plan changes.

Track this page regularly so your plan always matches current business needs.
