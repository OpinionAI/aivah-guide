# Slide Decks

Generate ready-to-present slide decks from any agent's knowledge base. Aivah pulls the source material the agent was trained on, structures it into a slide narrative, and renders the result so you can review, download, or push it into a live conversation.

Open from `Side nav → Productivity` (`/productivity`) and pick **Slide deck** in the **Type** selector.

## Configure

Click **Edit** in the productivity toolbar to open the slide-deck dialog.

| Setting | Options | What it controls |
|---|---|---|
| **Style** | Academic, Business, Casual | Visual tone and copy density. Academic prioritises citations and dense slides; Business uses concise, executive-style framing; Casual is friendlier and more visual. |
| **Length** | Short (5–8 slides), Medium, Long | Approximate slide count. Short is great for an intro or recap; Long covers comprehensive walkthroughs. |

Confirm to save the configuration, then click **Generate** in the toolbar.

## Workflow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick the source agent">
    Use the **Agent** selector. The infinite-scroll search makes it easy to find the right one even in large libraries.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose style and length">
    Click **Edit**, set **Style** and **Length**, then close the dialog.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate">
    Click **Generate**. The agent enters a generation state and the **Document Creating Banner** appears in any active conversation tied to that agent.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review in the gallery">
    Newly generated decks appear in the gallery under the toolbar. Click any slide to:
    - Open it full-screen
    - Download the deck
    - Push the slide into your active conversation as context
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Where decks live

- **Productivity gallery** – the fastest view, scoped to the active agent.
- **Integrations → AI Drive → Files → Slides** – global, browsable across every agent.
- **Avatar chat** – pick the same agent and open it in a presentation scene (Present 1 / Present 2) to deliver the deck with a 3D avatar.

## Best practices

- **Train the agent thoroughly first** – decks reflect the depth of the underlying knowledge. Add the relevant URLs, PDFs, and notes before generating.
- **Use Business style for stakeholders** – it produces the cleanest, most presentation-friendly output.
- **Iterate on length** – generate Short for a snapshot, then Long for a full readout once the structure feels right.
- **Pair with [Mind maps](/productivity/mind-maps)** – they're a great way to visualize the relationships between slides before formal delivery.
